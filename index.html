A little bit about the person behind the puzzles

Meet Bethany

Bethany is the kind of person who is hard not to love. She's smart, resourceful, wonderfully happy, and has a curiosity that keeps her mind constantly working.

Outside of her puzzle adventures, Bethany's happiest spending time with her family, her dogs Auggie and Rucca, playing board games, watching reality TV, singing in the shower, and, of course, enjoying anything Taylor Swift.

She's also someone who believes that having an open and accepting mind matters. Kindness matters. Being good to people matters. And she has little patience for people who are mean or rude.

Why Bethany Does Things

Bethany started making videos because she wanted an outlet to share something she genuinely loves — puzzles — with other people who enjoy them too.

And there's one thing that makes creating those videos even better:

Your comments.

A kind message can make Bethany's day. But if you really want to make things interesting, try beating her puzzle time.
